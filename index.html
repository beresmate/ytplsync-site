ytplsync

A personal tool that keeps a FreeTube playlist and a YouTube playlist in sync.

ytplsync runs on the user's own Windows computer. It provides a watch-later queue that works across devices: videos added to or removed from a playlist in the FreeTube desktop app are added to or removed from a private playlist on the user's YouTube account, and the other way around.

What it does with your YouTube account

  • Finds or creates the playlist you configured (private by default).
  • Reads the videos in that playlist.
  • Adds and removes videos in that playlist to match FreeTube.

It doesn't touch your other playlists, subscriptions, comments or channel. There is no ytplsync server: everything runs locally and talks directly to the YouTube Data API.
